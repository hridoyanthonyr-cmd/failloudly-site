INC-001 · community nonprofit · zoho crm
Pledges were being forgotten
resolved›
Symptom

Donations tracked differently depending on who entered them. Pledges sat at 90% forever because the follow-up date lived in someone's head.

Diagnosis

One pipeline doing three jobs. No stage meant "promised but unpaid", so nothing could chase itself and every forecast was wrong.

Fix

Three linked pipelines that hand records to each other. A pledge now creates its donation record, chases itself a week before the due date, and marks itself lapsed after thirty days unpaid.

INC-002 · roofing contractor · smartsuite
A scheduling job creating a duplicate every night
was criticalresolved›
Symptom

Records multiplying. Automations referencing fields that no longer existed. Two rules writing to the same field in an order nobody controlled.

Diagnosis

Years of additions with no audit. The record-creating automations had no lookup in front of them, so every re-run made another copy.

Fix

Audited every automation across three solutions, removed the broken and the redundant, made creation idempotent — update if it exists, create if it doesn't — and documented what remained in a register the client can read.
